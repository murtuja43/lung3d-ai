Everything You Need

A complete multimodal AI pipeline from X-ray to clinical report

🧠
ResNet18 CNN
Transfer learning on 800+ real chest X-rays. Trained to 85% accuracy on the Montgomery & Shenzhen TB datasets.
🔥
Grad-CAM Heatmaps
Visual explanation of AI decisions. See exactly which lung regions the model identified as suspicious — overlaid on the original X-ray.
👤
Patient Parameters
11 clinical parameters including age, BMI, symptoms, and risk factors fused with image analysis for higher accuracy predictions.
🌐
3D Lung Visualization
Interactive Three.js 3D point cloud of lung tissue. Abnormal regions highlighted in red, rotating in real time.

PDF Clinical Report
Download a professional clinical report with patient data, X-ray images, heatmap, prediction breakdown, and reasoning.

Real-time Analysis
